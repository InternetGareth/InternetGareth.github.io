I lead teams that develop and deliver AI and Machine Learning applications in the energy sector. I specialize in rapid-growth startups, focusing on enabling execution, building team capacity, and integrating with product strategy and roadmaps.

## Executive leadership

- **Board and investor materials**: Presenting technical deep-dives to investors and stakeholders at Series A, B, and C funding rounds.
- **Thought leadership**: Contributing technical perspective on the role of AI in the energy transition, including published commentary on applying deep learning to solar asset performance.
- **Long-term roadmap and resource planning**: Translating company goals into a multi-year data science and ML strategy, setting priorities and measuring impact against KPIs.
- **Energy and utility policy and regulation**: Before my life in tech, I worked as a senior consultant for a multinational design and engineering firm, WS Atkins, in their utility transaction advisory division in the London office. I contributed to technical and financial buyer- and vendor-side due diligence reports, and worked with national regulators, utilities, and policy makers across North Africa and Eastern Europe to deliver regulatory reform, infrastructure investment plans, and economic impact assessments.

## Technical leadership

I act as technical lead for teams delivering the following core capabilities:

- **Data infrastructure**: Defining data product requirements and pipeline SLAs; design and review of pipeline architecture and implementation; pipeline monitoring and maintenance.
- **Data governance**: Coordinating company-wide data governance principles and mechanisms, including data dictionaries, mediation schemas, and business term definitions.
- **MLOps and DevOps**: Mapping, formalizing, and automating model and software development workflows.
- **Full-stack AI infrastructure**: Oversight of the development, maintenance, and uptime of a self-hosted GPU server cluster.
- **Edge computing**: Interfacing with edge device teams to deliver edge inference products and solutions.

## Research and development

Deployed machine learning solutions, end to end:

- **Team leadership**: Managed and mentored data science teams, growing capabilities from inaugural ML services to comprehensive AI-driven solutions.
- **Intellectual property**: Inventor on provisional patents in areas including hardware specifications modeling and energy production monitoring.
- **Deployed and measured value**: Built and shipped models with measurable operational impact at fleet scale — including deep learning models that recognize regional soiling patterns and monitor soiling rates across more than 70,000 solar sites in near real-time.
- **Productization**: Partnered with product and engineering to take models from research prototype to production service, including real-time performance diagnostics and edge-deployed inference on grid hardware.
